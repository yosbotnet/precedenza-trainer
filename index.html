The order of authority

Police officer > traffic lights > signs > general rules. A flashing amber light counts as no light at all: use the signs or the general rules.

1 · Signs decide first

SignMeaning
🔻 Dare precedenza (dashed line + painted triangle)Give way to everyone on the road you're entering, from both sides. You only stop if needed.
🛑 STOP (solid line + "STOP" painted on the road)Full stop at the line, every time, even if the road is empty (wheels completely still). Then give way to everyone.
⚠️ Intersezione con diritto di precedenza / ◆ Strada con diritto di precedenzaYou're on the main road, so the side roads give way to you. Between cars on the main road, rule 3 still applies.

2 · No signs → precedenza a destra

Give way to whoever comes from your right. You have priority over whoever comes from your left. If every car has someone on its right (deadlock), the drivers sort it out by eye contact and one goes first, carefully.

3 · Turning left → let oncoming traffic go

When you turn left, you also give way to oncoming traffic that goes straight or turns right. This applies on a green light too! If two cars facing each other both turn left, they go together, passing in front of each other (art. 154: turn "close to the centre and to the left of it").

4 · Traffic lights

  • Green: you may go, but still give way to pedestrians on the crossing you turn across, and (when turning left) to oncoming traffic.
  • Amber: stop, unless you're so close that you can't stop safely. If you're standing at the line, you stay there.
  • Red: stop at the line.

5 · Pedestrians, ambulances, queues

  • Zebra crossing (strisce pedonali): give way to pedestrians who are crossing or about to cross, including when you turn into a road with a crossing. Never stop your car on the stripes.
  • Ambulance / police with siren and blue lights: everybody lets them through, whatever the signs say.
  • Coming out of a driveway, car park, petrol station or dirt road: give way to everyone.
  • Trams have priority at unmarked junctions, even from your left.

6 · Roundabouts (rotatoria)

Nearly all have Dare precedenza at the entry, so cars already circulating go first. They come from your left. Don't enter if there's no room to keep moving.

Reading the car coming from your left: right blinker and it hasn't reached your road's exit yet → it's leaving into your road, so you can go (check that its wheels turn out). No blinker, left blinker, or right blinker but already past your road's exit → it passes in front of you, so wait.

8 · Exam habits

  • Look left – right – left. At roundabouts, start looking left while you're still approaching.
  • Too much hesitation is also an error. When it's clearly your turn, go decisively.
  • Not sure the other driver has seen you? Slow down and make eye contact. You can only give priority, never take it.
