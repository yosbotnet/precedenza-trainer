The order of authority

Police officer > traffic light > signs > general rules. Everything below assumes no lights and no police.

1 · Signs decide first

SignMeaning
🔻 Dare precedenza (upside-down triangle; dashed line + painted triangle)Slow down and give way to everyone on the road you are entering, from both sides. You don't have to stop if it's clear.
🛑 STOP (solid line + "STOP" on the road)A full stop at the line every time, even when it's empty (wheels completely still). Then give way to everyone. At the exam, a rolling stop is an error.
⚠️ Intersezione con diritto di precedenza (triangle with a cross) / ◆ Strada con diritto di precedenzaYou are on the main road. Cars on the side roads give way to you. Between two cars on the main road, rule 3 still applies.

2 · No signs → precedenza a destra

Give way to anyone coming from your right. You have priority over anyone coming from your left.

3 · Turning left → let oncoming traffic go

If you turn left, you also give way to the car coming toward you when it goes straight or turns right. If you and the oncoming car both turn left, you can go together: each of you turns before the centre of the intersection, passing in front of the other (art. 154: turn "close to the centre and to the left of it").

4 · Special cases

  • Coming out of a driveway, a car park, a petrol station or a dirt road: give way to everyone.
  • Trams (veicoli su rotaia) have priority at unmarked junctions, even when they come from your left.
  • Emergency vehicles with siren + blue lights: always let them through.

5 · Roundabouts (rotatoria)

Almost all Italian roundabouts have a Dare precedenza sign at the entry, so cars already circulating go first. Traffic goes anticlockwise, so they come from your left. (Old roundabouts with no triangle at the entry follow precedenza a destra. They're rare; ask your instructor if your exam area has one.)

Your own blinker (what autoscuole teach and examiners expect):

Your exitEnteringBefore exiting
1st exit (right)➡ right➡ keep it on
Straight on (2nd)nothing➡ right, after passing the exit before yours
Left / beyond halfway / U-turn⬅ leftswitch to ➡ right after passing the exit before yours

Reading the car coming from your left:

  • ➡ Right blinker, and it hasn't reached your road's exit yet → it's probably leaving into your road. It doesn't cross your entry, so you can go. Confirm it: its nose and wheels turn outward.
  • No blinker or ⬅ left blinker → it stays in and will pass in front of you. Wait.
  • ➡ Right blinker but already past your road's exit → it's going to the next exit, so it passes in front of you. Wait.

Quick checks: a car from your right shows you its left side, so look for its front corners. "No blinker" usually means straight on, but people forget their blinker, so watch the wheels and the car's line too.

7 · Exam habits

  • Look left – right – left before you go. At a roundabout, start looking left while you're still approaching, so you've already picked your gap when you reach the line.
  • Hesitating too much is also an error. If the gap is clearly safe, go decisively.
  • Not sure whether the other driver has seen you? Slow down and make eye contact. You can only give priority; you can never take it.
